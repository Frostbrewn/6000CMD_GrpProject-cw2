Using SecureMarket safely

Most account takeovers come from weak or reused passwords and phishing. These habits prevent most of them.

Use a long, unique password

  • Length matters most. A passphrase of four random words is strong and easy to remember.
  • Never reuse a password from another site. If that site is breached, attackers will try it here.
  • A password manager can generate and store passwords for you.

Spot phishing

  • Before logging in, check the address bar shows the correct address and a valid HTTPS padlock.
  • Be wary of messages that create urgency ("your account will be closed") or ask you to log in through a link.
  • We will never ask for your password by email or chat.

Protect your session

  • Log out when you finish, especially on shared or public computers.
  • Sessions expire automatically after a period of inactivity.

Trade safely

  • Do not put your phone number, address or bank details in a listing description.
  • Be cautious of buyers or sellers who pressure you to pay before meeting or to move to another platform.
