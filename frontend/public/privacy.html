Privacy notice

Last updated: 12 November 2026. SecureMarket is a coursework demonstration system. This notice follows the principles of Malaysia's Personal Data Protection Act 2010 (PDPA).

1. What we collect

  • Account data: your name, email address and role. Your password is stored only as a one-way hash, never in readable form.
  • Listings: the title, description, category and price of items you advertise.
  • Activity records: security-relevant events such as registration, logins, failed login attempts and changes to listings, kept to protect accounts and investigate misuse.

We do not collect payment details, home addresses or phone numbers. Please do not put personal contact details in your listings.

2. Why we use it

  • To run your account and display your listings.
  • To keep the service secure: detecting repeated login attempts, investigating incidents and enforcing access rules.

We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

3. Who can see it

  • The public can see active listings.
  • Administrators can see account details, all listings and activity records in order to manage the service.
  • Your password is never visible to anyone, including administrators.

4. How we protect it

  • Traffic between your browser and the application is encrypted with HTTPS.
  • Passwords are stored using a slow, salted password-hashing algorithm.
  • Access is restricted by role and by ownership: only you can change your listings.
  • Error messages do not reveal system details, and activity records never contain passwords or session tokens.

5. Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data by contacting the administrator below.
